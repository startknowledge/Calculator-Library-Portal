Introduction to the Library Campaign

The Calgary Public Library's campaign is focused on raising funds to support its various programs and services, including literacy programs, author readings, and community events. By donating to the campaign, Calgarians can help ensure that the library continues to provide high-quality services and programs to its users. Why Donate to the Library Campaign?

Donating to the library campaign is essential for several reasons. Firstly, it helps to support the library's programs and services, which have a positive impact on the community. Secondly, it helps to ensure that the library remains a vibrant and inclusive community hub, providing access to information and resources for all. Finally, donating to the campaign is a way for Calgarians to give back to their community and support a cause that is close to their hearts. As Mayor Jyoti Gondek stated, "The Calgary Public Library is a vital part of our city's fabric, and I encourage all Calgarians to support this campaign" (City of Calgary, 2022).

Actionable Strategies for Donating to the Library Campaign

Here are 10 actionable strategies for Calgarians to donate to the library campaign:

  • Make a one-time donation: Calgarians can make a one-time donation to the library campaign by visiting the library's website or by mail. Donations can be made using a credit card, cheque, or money order.
  • Set up a monthly donation: Calgarians can set up a monthly donation to the library campaign, which will help to provide ongoing support to the library's programs and services.
  • Donate in honor of someone: Calgarians can make a donation to the library campaign in honor of someone, such as a loved one or a friend. This is a great way to recognize someone's love of reading or their support for the library.
  • Donate through a corporate matching program: Many companies offer corporate matching programs, which match employee donations to charitable causes. Calgarians can check with their employer to see if they offer a corporate matching program.
  • Volunteer at the library: Calgarians can volunteer at the library, which will help to support the library's programs and services. Volunteers can assist with tasks such as shelving books, helping with programs, and providing customer service.
  • Attend a library event: Calgarians can attend a library event, such as an author reading or a book sale, which will help to raise funds for the library campaign.
  • Donate books or materials: Calgarians can donate books or materials to the library, which will help to support the library's collections and programs.
  • Spread the word: Calgarians can spread the word about the library campaign by sharing information with friends, family, and social media networks.
  • Make a planned gift: Calgarians can make a planned gift to the library campaign, such as a bequest or a trust, which will help to provide long-term support to the library.
  • Donate through a fundraising event: Calgarians can participate in a fundraising event, such as a charity run or a bake sale, which will help to raise funds for the library campaign.
