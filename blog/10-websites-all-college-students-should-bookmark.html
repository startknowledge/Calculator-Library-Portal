2. Khan Academy: Free Learning and Practice Tools

While not a calculator per se, Khan Academy is a cornerstone resource for college students struggling with course material. Founded in 2008 by educator Salman Khan, this platform offers thousands of free video lessons, practice exercises, and interactive tools across subjects like math, science, economics, and even test prep (e.g., SAT, GRE).

Why Students Love It

  • Self-Paced Learning: Unlike rigid lecture schedules, Khan Academy lets you review topics at your own speed.
  • Adaptive Practice: The platform adjusts difficulty based on your performance, ensuring you master foundational concepts before moving on.
  • Test Prep Resources: The SAT, ACT, and AP practice tests are invaluable for standardized exam prep.
  • Mobile-Friendly: Access lessons on the go via the Khan Academy app.

How to Use It Like a Pro

  1. Bookmark the “College Prep” Section: This hub includes calculus, linear algebra, and economics courses tailored to college-level rigor.
  2. Use the “Practice” Mode: After watching a video, test your understanding with interactive questions. The platform provides instant feedback and explanations.
  3. Leverage the “Mission” Feature: Set custom learning goals (e.g., “Master differential equations”) and track progress.
  4. Avoid Common Mistakes:
    • Don’t skip the practice exercises. Watching videos alone won’t reinforce learning.
    • Avoid treating it as a replacement for lectures. Use it to supplement your coursework, not replace it.
    • Don’t neglect the “Community Questions” forum for help with tricky problems.
